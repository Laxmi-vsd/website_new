## About Me

I am a postgraduate student specializing in **VLSI Physical Design and Embedded Systems** at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Bhubaneswar. My focus is on the complete digital IC implementation flow — from RTL synthesis through placement, clock tree synthesis, routing, and signoff.

I have hands-on experience with industry-standard EDA tools including **Cadence Innovus**, **Synopsys PrimeTime**, **Magic VLSI**, and the open-source **OpenLane/OpenROAD** flow. My work spans both academic coursework and practical lab-based projects on real VLSI design challenges.

My career goal is to join a leading semiconductor company as a **Physical Design Engineer** or **ASIC Design Engineer**, contributing to the development of high-performance, low-power silicon.
