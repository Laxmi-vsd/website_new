## ICCAD 2026 Paper Reviews

As part of coursework in VLSI Physical Design at IIT Bhubaneswar, I produced structured academic peer reviews for papers submitted to **ICCAD 2026** (IEEE/ACM International Conference on Computer-Aided Design).

---

### ERL-Placer: Entropy-Regularized Learning-Based Placer

!!! info "Review Summary"
    Reviewed a machine learning-based placement algorithm leveraging entropy regularization for improved wirelength and routability tradeoffs in large-scale VLSI placement.

**Topic:** ML-guided placement, reinforcement learning, EDA automation  
**Conference:** ICCAD 2026  
**Review Type:** Structured academic peer review (DOCX format)

---

### GTStress: Gradient-Driven Thermal Stress-Aware Physical Design

!!! info "Review Summary"

**Topic:** Thermal stress, 3D IC, advanced nodes, physical design  
**Conference:** ICCAD 2026  
**Review Type:** Structured academic peer review (DOCX format)

**Deliverables:** Matplotlib figures, interactive HTML dashboards, Excel data files
